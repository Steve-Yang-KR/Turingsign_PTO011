# Principal #001 Runbook — Bay Area Alpha

## Objective
Use the Personal Trust Office personally during the Stanford/Bay Area period to test whether bounded, revocable authority reduces human approval burden without surrendering control.

## Week 1: synthetic only
- Initialize Principal #001 and MFA.
- Exercise all Financial / Health / Legal sample actions.
- Confirm every APPROVE action appears in the Approval Inbox.
- Confirm DENY hard guardrails cannot be changed.
- Create at least 10 signed Trust Receipts.
- Test Health → Financial minimum-disclosure delegation.

## Week 2: low-risk personal workflow
- Store only non-sensitive Alpha notes in the encrypted vault.
- Prepare calendar events through the Health Agent.
- Prepare `.eml` drafts through the Legal Agent; review manually before sending.
- Adjust one policy threshold and verify that a broader threshold requires fresh MFA.

## Week 3+: controlled daily use
Track:
- total Action Intents
- AUTO / NOTIFY / APPROVE / DENY counts
- time to human decision
- overrides or policy corrections
- unnecessary escalations
- any unexpected data disclosure

## Principal #001 KPI
**Approval Compression Ratio = consequential Action Intents / explicit human approvals.**

The Alpha is useful only if routine, policy-compliant actions cease to require repetitive human approvals while exceptions still surface reliably.

## Do not enter yet
- brokerage credentials or trade execution permissions
- medical treatment/medication instructions
- privileged attorney-client production records unless counsel and security review permit it
- Social Security numbers, full bank credentials, private keys or production API secrets
